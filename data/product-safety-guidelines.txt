# Product Safety Compliance Guidelines

## Battery Safety Requirements

### Button-Cell Batteries

Button-cell batteries pose a significant safety hazard if ingested by children. Products containing button-cell batteries must meet the following requirements:

1. **Secure Compartments**: Battery compartments must be secured with a screw or require the use of a tool to open. Products with easily accessible button-cell batteries are prohibited from sale.

2. **Warning Labels**: Products must display clear warning labels about battery ingestion hazards. Labels must be visible and legible, stating: "DANGER: Swallowing can lead to serious injury or death in as little as 2 hours due to chemical burns."

3. **Testing Requirements**: All products must undergo testing to verify that the battery compartment cannot be opened by a child without tools.

### Lithium Ion Batteries

Lithium ion battery packs must comply with international safety standards to prevent thermal runaway events:

- Battery packs must include overcharge protection circuits
- Thermal fuses must activate at temperatures above 85°C
- Products must pass drop test requirements without battery damage
- Charging circuits must comply with IEC 62368-1 standards

## Window Covering Safety

Corded window coverings present a strangulation hazard to children. All window covering products must meet one of the following criteria:

1. **Cordless Design**: The preferred solution. Window coverings should use cordless lift mechanisms such as spring-loaded systems or motorized controls.

2. **Short Cord Design**: If cords are present, they must be short enough (less than 6 inches of exposed cord) to prevent loop formation.

3. **Breakaway Feature**: Continuous loop cords must include a tension device that breaks away under 6 pounds of force.

4. **Warning Labels**: All corded products must include warning labels showing the strangulation hazard and instructions for safe installation away from cribs and furniture children can climb.

Products failing to meet these requirements will be removed from sale immediately.

## Textile Flammability Requirements

### Children's Sleepwear

Children's sleepwear in sizes 9 months through size 14 must meet strict flammability standards under 16 CFR 1615 and 1616.

**Tight-Fitting Exemption**: Garments may be exempt from flame-resistant treatment if they:
- Pass specific dimensional requirements for tight fit
- Include labels stating "WEAR SNUG-FITTING. NOT FLAME RESISTANT."
- Do not exceed specified body measurements for each size

**Flame-Resistant Treatment**: Sleepwear not meeting tight-fit criteria must:
- Be inherently flame-resistant (polyester fleece is acceptable)
- Maintain flame resistance after 50 wash cycles
- Pass vertical flame tests with char length not exceeding 7 inches

### General Textile Requirements

All textile products must:
- Be free from hazardous substances above regulated limits
- Include accurate fiber content labels
- Provide care instructions in the primary language of the target market

## Small Parts Requirements

Products intended for children under 3 years must not contain small parts that pose a choking hazard.

**Testing Protocol**: The small parts test cylinder (31.7mm diameter × 57.1mm depth) determines whether a part is too small. Any component that fits entirely within this cylinder is considered a small part and is prohibited.

**Exemptions**: Books and other paper products are exempt from small parts regulations, though they must still meet general safety requirements.

## General Safety Requirements

All consumer products must:
1. Include safety warnings in the primary language of the target market
2. Provide accurate age grading based on safety considerations
3. Meet applicable federal safety standards for the product category
4. Include manufacturer contact information for consumer questions
5. Undergo third-party testing when required by regulation

Warnings must be:
- Legible and durable
- Positioned where consumers will see them before use
- Written in clear, understandable language

Non-compliance with these requirements will result in immediate product removal and potential recalls.
